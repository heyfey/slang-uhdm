r10uvm128 — THE GRID.  Five arms, DEF and ELAB, scored against GOLDEN with
score128.py (a SPAN-and-NAME-keyed multiset census: one row per `operation`
carrying its opType, span and OPERAND COUNT, one row per `hier_path` carrying
its name, span and ordered element list; no ids, no file, no VpiFullName).

LHS is ALWAYS golden (Surelog); RHS is ALWAYS ours.

WHY THE MACROS LIVE IN THEIR OWN FILE (g128_defs.svh).  The law's gate is that
the two halves of a written receiver come from TWO DIFFERENT BUFFERS.  A macro
DEFINED AND INVOKED IN ONE FILE does not have that shape — both ends map back
into the same file and `rawText` returns a long garbage substring instead — so
a same-file arm reproduces the SYMPTOM through a DIFFERENT MECHANISM and tests
nothing.  The first cut of arm A was written that way and DID show twelve
operations with one operand where golden has two; instrumenting it showed the
law never fired.  UVM's layout is the cross-file one, and so is the grid's.

WHY THE MACRO BODIES START ON A CONTINUATION LINE.  A body that begins on the
`define line itself lands in `macroInvLineCol`'s SINGLE-LINE regime, where our
spans map to the INVOCATION line while golden maps to the DEFINITION line — a
PRE-EXISTING residue that would leave every cell unmatchable and make the arm
unable to show convergence.  Arms B/C/D were first written that way; the whole
grid was renormalised to UVM's own layout (body on continuation lines).

WHY THE NAMES HAVE FOUR DIFFERENT LENGTHS.  The macro formal is `ARG`, three
characters, and the arguments are 1, 5, 12 and 20 characters.  A cell that
merely walked `start + name.size()` could not agree with golden by accident:
the column shift the argument paste causes is different in every cell.  That is
what caught the element END span (column 78 where golden writes 69) before the
law shipped.

READING THE ROWS.  SAME grows and ONLY-GOLDEN shrinks in EVERY arm and BOTH
views; ONLY-OURS never grows except in arm A, where it grows by exactly the six
hier_paths that the pristine emitted with a GARBAGE NAME (a name containing a
newline, which the census cannot key at all — the UNPARSEABLE counter is those
continuation lines: arm A def 236 -> 85, elab 419 -> 117, golden unchanged at
50 / 78).  Those six rows are not new wrongness; they are six objects that were
unrowable garbage and are now well-formed, differing from golden in ONE field.

THE ONE FIELD, and it is PRE-EXISTING, not this law's (README residue R1): the
`method_func_call` element's END span, `mStart + name.size()` where `mStart` is
`recv->sourceRange.end() + 1`.  That is a raw offset walk past the end of a
one-token macro-ARGUMENT buffer; `macroInvLineCol`'s newline guard then refuses
and `setLoc` falls back to the invocation site.  It is computed IDENTICALLY for
the FIRST operand of every cell in these arms, whose hier_path this law never
touches, and every one of those is wrong in the pristine too.  ON THE CORPUS IT
DOES NOT BITE — the invocation line `\`uvm_field_queue_string(abstractions,
UVM_DEFAULT)` is long enough that the walk stays on it — and the corpus head
object is BYTE-IDENTICAL to golden, field for field.
