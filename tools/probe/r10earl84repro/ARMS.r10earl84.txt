and with BOTH FROZEN binaries, censuses all three with ct84cen --inst, and
score3_84.py scores every cell three ways.  Every child gets </dev/null and
its rc is RECORDED.  --inst puts the owning module_inst's VpiFullName in the
KEY: without it a multiset join pairs the fold in ONE elaborated copy against
golden's value in ANOTHER and manufactures a phantom WRONG->OTHER (it did,
on g4's three mid84 instances, before the key was added).
