r10uvm138 — THE BOUNDARY GATE (bound138.sh).

Every .sv and .v in a COPY of tools/probe (top level AND subdirectories),
compiled in BOTH views with the pristine and the patched binary.  The test
is DATABASE BYTE IDENTITY plus rc equality — strictly stronger than a
cmp-line count.  The file list is read on fd 3, every child gets
</dev/null, scratch is keyed on the RELATIVE path (three arms in this tree
are all named dut.sv), and PROCESSED is asserted against LISTED.

POSITIVE CONTROL: this pass's own g138_*.sv.  A first cut of the gate
passed a '-sv' flag our binary does not accept and produced NO databases
at all while printing MOVED_ARMS=0; the g138 arms failing to appear is what
convicted it.  PRODUCED_DBS is now counted and printed, and it is what a
reader should check before believing any of the numbers below.
