== PART 4 — THE `this` NET, AND WHY IT IS NOT A PHANTOM IN THE CORPUS

  The pristine head's prefix ref_obj BINDS a module net called `this`
  (`logic_net work@top_tb.this` @152:45, read off the ladder's own R1 dump on
  the control SeqDriver).  In the 20-line repro g85_a8_corpus.sv that net is
  MINTED FOR THAT REFERENCE ALONE and golden has none: pristine THISNET 1,
  golden 0, patched 0 — the mis-bind and the net both go with the law.
  IN THE CORPUS THE NET IS GOLDEN'S TOO: SeqDriver and SimpleUVM have exactly
  ONE each on BOTH sides (golden @902:36, ours @152:45 — a SPAN difference,
  and vpiFile/span are not compared), and Scoreboard / BuildUVMPkg / MiniAmiq
  have NONE on either side.  After the patch THISNET is UNCHANGED in all nine
  databases: the corpus net has other unresolvable `this` references keeping it
  alive, so removing this one reference removed no object.  A law that had
  deleted golden's net would have shown up here as 1 -> 0.

== PART 5 — THE ARITY RULE AT SCALE
  GARGS moves 339 -> 387 (SeqDriver/MiniAmiq), 217 -> 241 (SimpleUVM/
  Scoreboard), 217 -> 237 (BuildUVMPkg): +48 / +24 / +20 arguments over +40 /
  +20 / +16 calls.  More arguments than calls, because a converted call may
  carry two; and NOT one per call, because a chain two names below the handle
  keeps none (arm a6 L21/L22, arm a4 L26).  A law that had kept every argument
  would have overshot here, and one that had dropped them all would have
  undershot by 48.
