R0 (corrected): a reference to a subroutine local written as an ARGUMENT of a
method_func_call on a hierarchical receiver is UNBOUND in golden -- the ref_obj carries
no vpiActual, and its (dump-only) fullname is scoped under the CALL
(...send_request::m_req_fifo.try_put::param_t), not under the subroutine.  We bind it to
the local's ref_var.  The judged field is ref_obj::Actual_group, the SINGULAR overload of
RESUME §9 (buvm13/15's shape), which is why the head prints 'LHS: <null>' with a full RHS.
Deleting the Variables entry, as r10uvm177's R0 directs, would move a cell AWAY from golden.
