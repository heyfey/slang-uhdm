2.  DECODING SURELOG + UHDM — WHY ONE SLOT BINDS AND THE OTHER DOES NOT
================================================================================
1. `CompileHelper::importPackage`
   (Surelog src/DesignCompile/CompileHelper.cpp:318-327): when the importer is
   a PACKAGE (`inPackage`), every imported task/function is
   `UHDM::clone_tree`'d.  An importing MODULE / PROGRAM / `$unit` pushes the
   HOME OBJECT itself (`sfuncs->push_back(func)`, :326) — no clone, nothing to
   unbind.  That is arm D's control, and it moves zero bytes.
2. `task::DeepClone` (UHDM build/generated/src/clone_tree.cpp:611-708) clones
   `Io_decls()` and then **`Variables()` at :634-641** — and only calls
   `enterTask_func(clone, …)` at **:660**.  So while the SUBROUTINE-LEVEL
   declarations are cloned the elaborator's scope stack holds NO frame for the
   subroutine.  `ref_var::DeepCopy`
   (UHDM build/generated/src/ref_var.cpp:75-83) is
       if (!clone->Actual_group())
           clone->Actual_group(elaboratorContext->m_elaborator.bindAny(VpiName()));
       if (!clone->Actual_group())
           clone->Actual_group((any*) Actual_group());
   `bindAny` (ElaboratorListener.cpp:899) walks that stack, misses, and the
   fallback copies the SOURCE's `Actual_group` — NULL on a home declaration.
   THE SUBROUTINE'S OWN LISTING ENTRY IS UNBOUND.
3. `task_func::DeepCopy` (build/generated/src/task_func.cpp:120) then clones
   `Stmt()`.  `begin::DeepClone` (build/generated/src/begin.cpp:71-81) opens
   with `enterBegin(this, …)` — on the **SOURCE** begin, whose `Variables()`
   are the HOME objects (ElaboratorListener.cpp:1193-1213 builds the varMap
   from them).  Every body-level `ref_var` clone therefore binds to the HOME
   object.  THE BODY LISTING IS BOUND — which § pkgCloneLocalRefBind37 already
   reproduces.

Why a `process` local is a `ref_var` at all is § builtinClassLocalUnsupported-
BeforeClassPhase115's, not this law's: Surelog registers its builtin
`process`/`semaphore`/`mailbox` classes AFTER the package pass, so a package or
out-of-body-method body local of that type falls to `compileVariable`'s
`ref_var` arm (CompileType.cpp:421-431) over an `unsupported_typespec` named
for the written token.  This law is KIND-BLIND: it reaches every `ref_var` a
clone's own listing holds, whatever minted it (arm C's § 155 statement-typedef
local moves identically).

3.  DECODING SLANG — WHERE OURS BOUND IT
================================================================================
`UhdmExporter.cpp`'s § pkgCloneLocalRefBind37 sweep
(`for (auto& kv37 : s.AllObjects()) if (bindable37(o37)) bind9(o37, o37);`)
binds EVERY `ref_var` in a clone body by name, including the DECLARATION
object, and our emitter puts that ONE object in both the subroutine's and the
body block's `Variables()`.  Both of golden's slots are therefore served by one
bound object, and the subroutine's slot is wrong.

THE FIX IS THE OMISSION, and it runs LAST, after both bind sweeps: for every
wildcard-import clone subroutine, a slot of its OWN `Variables()` holding a
`ref_var` with a non-null `vpiActual` is unbound.  Where the object is listed
somewhere else (`GetByVpiType(vpiVariables)` membership > 1 — the body block)
the SLOT takes an unbound twin and the body keeps the bound object; where it is
listed nowhere else (the TF-ITEM production of § 139 builds no body `begin` at
all — arm A `tongs195`, `bellows195`) the one object simply loses the binding
and nothing is minted.
