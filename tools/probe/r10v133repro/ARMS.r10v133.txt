WHY A SECOND SCORER.  The pass's qjoin133.py calls a site BOTHKEEP as soon as
EITHER side has one kept call, so a site golden emits TWICE - once kept (the
`uhdmallModules` definition listing) and once folded (the `uhdmtopModules`
elaborated copy) - reads as a match when only one of the two copies agrees.
v133cen.py scores the EXACT PAIR (kept copies, folded copies) per side instead,
so a half-match is reported as MOVED-OTHER, not as TOGOLD.  v133reg.py is
cell-free: it diffs the three dumps as MULTISETS of indent-stripped lines and
reports LOST (a golden line pristine had and patched lost) and GAINED (a
non-golden line only patched produces) - the only two shapes a regression can
take that no cell key can hide.
