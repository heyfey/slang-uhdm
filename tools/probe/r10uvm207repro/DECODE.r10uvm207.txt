2.  SLANG-UHDM — WHERE THE MEMBER EMISSION RUNS AND WHY EVERY EXISTING WALK
    MISSES IT, AND WHY THIS ONE CANNOT BE A RE-POINT
================================================================================

`src/TypeEmit.cpp` § importCloneBareTdAlias16 reaches a clone body's typespec
refs by four routes today:

  * the clone `task_func`'s own `Io_decls()` vector;
  * its own `Variables()` vector;
  * § cloneBodyBareTdAlias180's `UhdmListener`, for a block-local and for an
    expression's typespec — its climb returns the moment
    `any_cast<const UHDM::typespec*>(cs[i])` succeeds, so a `struct_typespec`
    stops it exactly as an `array_typespec` did;
  * § cloneArrayElemBareTdAlias205's declaration walk, which descends
    `array_typespec` / `packed_array_typespec` `Elem_typespec` and
    `Index_typespec` links only.

A struct member sits one link below none of those.  But the reason this pass is
not "§ 205 with `.mem` added to the walk" is the SHARING, and it is measured:

  `ct207cen --decide`, SeqDriver/default, pristine
      DECIDE SUMMARY hot-holders=8 PURE(all reaches differ)=0 MIXED(some reach agrees)=8

  i.e. EVERY holder object carrying a differing member cell is also reached by
  home-package declarations whose member cells AGREE with golden.  Two of the
  eight are the struct typespecs of this family; six are the `array_typespec`
  holders of r10uvm205's R1.

  `ct207ref`, the same database, from the other side
      MEMBER REFS BY VERDICT:          MOVE=0  REFUSE-SHARED=5  HOME=5
      STRUCT-BINDING REFS BY VERDICT:  MOVE=22 REFUSE-SHARED=2  HOME=28

  A member's `ref_typespec` therefore can NEVER be re-pointed: one ref cannot
  carry two names and golden keeps the home cell QUALIFIED.  The object that
  CAN move is the ref that BINDS THE STRUCT — a declaration's own `Typespec()`
  or an array's `Elem_typespec` — re-pointed onto a CLONE COPY of the struct.
