ARM-DESIGN TRAPS THIS GRID PAYS (two inherited, two new)
  * INHERITED (r10uvm128repro/ARMS): a macro DEFINED AND INVOKED IN ONE FILE
    reproduces the symptom through a DIFFERENT mechanism, and a body that
    begins on the `define line lands in macroInvLineCol's SINGLE-LINE regime
    where no cell can converge.  Every definition lives in g132_defs.svh and
    every body begins on a continuation line.
  * NEW: a trailing `\` on the LAST line of the definitions file makes Surelog
    report `extraneous input '<' ... <EOF>` and emit NO database — the arm then
    reads as "NO GOLDEN", not as a failure.  g132_defs.svh ends with a comment.
  * NEW: Surelog's preprocessor cannot carry an ESCAPED IDENTIFIER (`\name `)
    through a `\`-continued macro body (three PA0207 syntax errors, no
    database), and slang's macro-argument parser rejects it as an ARGUMENT.
    The escaped-identifier fence therefore has NO golden and is arm G, scored
    by PRISTINE-vs-PATCHED dump identity plus the census instrument's `fit`
    column.
