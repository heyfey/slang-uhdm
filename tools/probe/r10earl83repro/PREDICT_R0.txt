    AND NOT BY US.  `g1_ctx83.sv:58` (`localparam int Q = integer'(7)`):

    a `ref_typespec -> int_typespec` (the parameter's own type), while its DEF
    copy stays the cast operation; we emit the operation in both views.  Note

R2  A CAST WIDTH OF 64 OR MORE IS SURELOG UB, AND IT IS OBSERVABLE.  Measured
    golden: `64'(64'hFFFF_FFFF_FFFF_FFFF)` is `sz64 UINT:0` and
    `96'(64'hFFFF_FFFF_FFFF_FFFF)` is `sz96 UINT:4294967295` — ExprEval's
    `mask = (1ULL << cast_to) - 1` shifts by >= 64, which x86 does modulo 64.
    `mask = (1ULL << (W % 64)) - 1` reproduces BOTH cells exactly.  This pass
    refuses W >= 64 rather than encode UB into the emitter; a future pass that
    wants those cells must decide whether to model the host's shift.

R3  GOLDEN REDUCES THE WHOLE ACTUAL, NOT JUST THE CAST.  In an ELAB High_conn
    golden folds an ENTIRE constant-valued expression to ONE constant: an
    arithmetic operation (`integer'(0) + 64'd2` -> `INT:2 sz64 dec"2"`, stamped
    at the CAST's span), a concatenation, an indexed-part-select over a
    constant base, a ternary with a constant condition (the false arm vanishes)
    and a `$clog2` call.  We fold only the cast inside them.  Measured on
    `g1_ctx83.sv` C2/C3/C5/C7/C8; ZERO corpus cells today (all six FAMILY cells
    are bare actuals), which is why this pass scores 1 WRONG->OTHER on C2 and
    nothing on the corpus.  A whole-actual reduce law would subsume it.
