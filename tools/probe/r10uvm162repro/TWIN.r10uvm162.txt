== r10uvm162 — THE ORACLE PROOF and the ARM CELL TABLE (twin162.py)

For every ok_/bd_ pair in ONE database: is the object inside the REJECTED
concatenation field-for-field the ACCEPTED twin's?  Run on GOLDEN it is the
PROOF that the accepted twin is an oracle for the recovery; run on OURS it
says which cells the law recovered and which it abstained on (an abstention
prints bd=4 lines — the empty operand vector).  Arms E, F and G do not pair
cell for cell by construction (arm E's contexts differ structurally, arms F
and G have no ok_/bd_ pairs at all) and GOLDEN itself reads NOTTWIN there;
they are scored by uhdm-cmp and by armscore162.py instead.
MEASUREMENT ONLY; the official uhdm-cmp is the judge.  LHS/G is ALWAYS golden.
