LAW A — § connOperandOwnScopeBind61   (ExprEmit netMap bind + ModuleEmit flag)
=====================================================================
In the ELABORATED view a NESTED reference inside a port HighConn expression
(anything below the top-level actual: an operation's operand, a select's index,
a call argument) binds its `Actual_group` ONLY to what the instantiation's OWN
scope declares.  At module scope that is the whole module body, so nothing
changes; inside a GENERATE BLOCK it is THAT BLOCK ALONE, and an outer name — the
enclosing module's port/net/var, or an OUTER generate block's local — stays
UNBOUND.  The TOP-LEVEL actual is exempt (Surelog binds it through `bind_net_`,
which walks the hierarchy).

LAW B — § patFlatUnsizedMemberWidth61   (ExprEmit pattern-flatten arm)
=====================================================================
On the CONT_ASSIGN carrier, an assignment-pattern member value written as an
UNSIZED literal (`'0`, `'1`, `'x`, `'z`) is re-typed to the MEMBER's own width:
`vpiSize` = member bits, `UINT:<0 or mask>`, `vpiConstType` 9, decompile the
decimal digits.  `'x`/`'z` fold to 0.  A SIZED literal, a bare decimal, and any
constant nested INSIDE a member value (a ternary arm) are untouched.  This is
the other half of the landed § patFlatDefaultFillMemberWidth27, whose "the
default setter only" scope note is hereby refuted by measurement.
