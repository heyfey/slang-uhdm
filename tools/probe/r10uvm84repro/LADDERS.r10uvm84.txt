The FULL AFTER ladder (MAX=20000 BUDGET=1800 on all seven rows) was launched and is
NOT reported here as rung TOTALS.  A concurrent agent's own ctladder run held the
machine for the whole window, and under that contention the AFTER SeqDriver row was
still climbing when this artifact was written, at which point it had walked
  276 rungs with THIS PASS'S FAMILY at ZERO of them (BEFORE: 494 rungs, 13 of them),
  after which the run was STOPPED rather than left to starve the machine further.

the other would be the "instrument's shipped numbers" error r10ladder1 was rejected
for.  What IS reported is complete and is the load-bearing half: the calibration, the
FULL before ladder, and R1 on BOTH sides for ALL SEVEN row-modes — the rung the
official judge stops at.
