== PART 1 — THE THREE POPULATIONS, and why they are three and not one
MOCKUP  a `constant` carrying ONLY VpiDecompile=="this" (size 0, const type 0, no
        typespec).  Surelog's CompileExpression.cpp:5250 "TODO: this is a mockup".
THISKW  a `constant` decompile "this" WITH size 4 / STRING:this / vpiStringConst(6):
        the ORDINARY `paThis_keyword` primary (the vpiPrefix of a STATEMENT-position
        `this.q.m(a);`).  THE CONTROL POPULATION — this pass must not move it.
CAND    a `hier_path` named `this…`/`super…` with TWO OR MORE dots: the shape OUR
        pristine emitter put where golden puts a MOCKUP.  Golden HAS legitimate CANDs
        (`this.q.len` for the written `this.q[i].len()`), which is why the deficit is
        a JOIN and not a count.
