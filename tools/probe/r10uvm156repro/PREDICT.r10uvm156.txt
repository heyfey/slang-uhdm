`movers156.py` runs the predicate over the UHDM PARENT CHAIN of the pristine
database, and the production predicate runs it over the SLANG SYMBOL's scope.
The two agree on WHICH DATABASE moves (5 / 956, and the byte verifier then
found exactly those five) but NOT on how many cells inside it: the parent-chain
reading counts our IMPORT-CLONE copies of a uvm_pkg out-of-body method that sit
under `package(adpcm_pkg)` as "far", while production asks the ORIGINAL slang
SubroutineSymbol, whose scope is still the uvm_pkg class, and leaves them
anonymous.  The measured cell counts are therefore the ones in
CENSUS.r10uvm156.txt (7 / 7 / 21 / 21 / 1 = 57), not the 222 / 243 / 2 here.

databases.
