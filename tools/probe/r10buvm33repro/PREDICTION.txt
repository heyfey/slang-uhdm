METHOD.  `r10buvm33cen` (family FEHDR) enumerates EVERY `foreach_stmt` in a
database with the object under `vpiVariables` — its kind, VpiName, span and
ACTUAL typespec — and marks `scoped=Y` when that name carries a `::`.
`sweepcen33.sh` ran it over BOTH sides of every cell of the pristine surelog
workdir (956 databases x 2 modes; 617 cells have a missing side and are
COUNTED, not skipped) and of the pristine uhdm2rtlil workdir (1358 cells).
`cen33.py` joins as a MULTISET keyed on (test, mode, foreach_stmt span, home) —
the array reference's NAME is deliberately OUT of the key, because the name is
exactly what the two producers disagree about and a name in the key would hide
every disagreement as a MISSING/EXTRA pair.
