r10uvm125 — THE CALL-ARGUMENT CENSUS.  LHS IS ALWAYS GOLDEN (Surelog);
RHS IS ALWAYS OURS.  MEASUREMENT ONLY — the official uhdm-cmp is the judge.

cen125 emits one CELL per Tf_call_args element of every tf_call in a database:
  SITE (the call's parent chain, kinds+names+spans, NO ids) | CALL (kind|name|
  span) | IDX | PAYLOAD (arg kind; for an operation its opType, operand count,
  inner kind, INNER-OPERAND PARENT KIND as its own column, spans) | formal
  DIRECTION (read off the callee's io_decls when the call carries a link).

Two joins are scored, both as MULTISETS (a last-writer dict drops rows):
  score125.py  keys on (SITE, CALL, IDX) — the fine key, parent chains included;
  wrap125.py   keys on (CALL, IDX) alone and reduces the payload to the shape
               this law is about, so it joins without depending on either
               producer's block spans.
