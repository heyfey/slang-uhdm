r11u80 SITE CENSUS — the movers PREDICTED BY NAME, a priori, from the corpora
themselves, with a POSITIVE CONTROL for every counter.

INSTRUMENT.  `cellcen80.py` replays `verify_golden.py`'s OWN discovery and its
OWN slang argument vector, runs the PATCHED binary with `R11U77_CELLDBG=1`,
and writes one row per traced site with that invocation's rc.  Two buckets are
read out of it:

  * the UNPACKED ROAD  — `R11U77HOIST` lines whose `kind=` is 5 (ArrayVar),
    6 (ArrayNet), 7 (ArraySkip) or 8 (ArrayTdEmpty).  Every entry LAW A can
    touch prints one, the declined ones included.
  * LAW B             — `R11U77DEFNT` lines that read MOVED, differenced
    against the same run under `R11U80_VARNT_OFF=1`.

THE POSITIVE CONTROLS, on the grid, with the SAME counter and the SAME
binary — both buckets fire:

  arm                unpacked road (kind=count)      defnt MOVED  on / off
  z1_arrgrid         k5=23  k6=5  k7=1  k8=1              9  /  10
  vh_arrelem         k5=6   k6=4                          6  /   6
  vb_unpackedfaces   k5=5   k6=1  k8=1                    5  /   5
  y2_pkgvarkind      k5=1   k6=1                          5  /   5
  z2_varnt           (none)                              10  /   7
  vi_defnt           (none)                               5  /   4
  g2_importshapes    (none)                               1  /   1

A census zero is therefore a reading, not a silence.

WHY `MOVED 0` SETTLES LAW B.  The `R11U77DEFNT` line prints the PRE-77 answer
and the CURRENT one side by side; LAW B can only turn a nonzero answer into 0,
so an entry it fires on with a nonzero pre-77 answer reads MOVED.  MOVED = 0
over both corpora therefore means the def-view `vpiNetType` byte of every
hoisted corpus entry is unchanged — no hoisted corpus declaration carries the
`var` qualifier.

WHY THE `kind` HISTOGRAM SETTLES LAW A.  Every entry the hoist loop considers
prints one `R11U77HOIST` line, the DECLINED ones included, and LAW A can only
act on kinds 5-8.  278 lines, 0 of them on the unpacked road, means no corpus
declaration reaches this builder at all.
