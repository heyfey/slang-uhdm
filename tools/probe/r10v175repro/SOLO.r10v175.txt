of a REGEX NIT in `score175.py`'s `judgeform`: `IDXSPAN` strips

for an UNPARENTED index, so `idx=constant@^-` (ours, the bare-constant arm's
deliberate no-vpiParent) fails to equal `idx=constant@^` (golden).  The nit
over-reports difference and can never hide an AWAY, so it does not weaken any
gate; it slightly under-counts the judge column's TOGOLD.
