========================================================================
`ct96cen` enumerates three families over EVERY database of both corpora, keyed
TOP-DOWN from a named root (module_inst / interface_inst / package) by the
FIELD PATH the walk took -- NEVER by vpiParent, because the two producers park
these objects on different parents and no generated Compare reads it:

  TS  every typespec reachable through Param_assigns (Lhs AND Rhs), Parameters,
      Nets, Variables and the root's own Typespecs, following
      ref_typespec->Actual_typespec, Elem_typespec and Typedef_alias.
      PAY = kind | VpiName | vpiInstance name   <- the fields Compare reads
  AL  the ALIAS-CHAIN projection: own name -> alias target name (+ whether the
      target is itself aliased).  This is the family the law moves.
  GA  the GLOBAL tripwire: EVERY named typespec in the arena keyed by kind +
      span + name, so a rename ANYWHERE -- inside the walked paths or outside
      them -- shows up as a bucket delta.

`score96.py` joins the two sides as a MULTISET (keys collide; a last-writer
dict drops rows).  The census was run over the PRISTINE sweeps and scored
BEFORE a line of the law was written.
