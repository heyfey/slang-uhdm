THE SIGNATURE, AND WHY IT IS EXACT
----------------------------------
The arm this pass narrows is the ONLY producer in this emitter of a
`method_func_call` whose `vpiPrefix` ref_obj is named from a RAW TEXT SLICE.
The pristine arm cuts that slice at the statement's LAST '.', so the two
readings — "last dot of the statement" and "last dot of the callee" —
disagree EXACTLY when a '(' precedes the last dot, and in that case the slice
necessarily CONTAINS that '('.  Therefore

    a `ref_obj` whose name contains '('   <=>   this arm mis-routed a call

and the census key is database-side, complete, and cheap.  Calibration:
golden holds ZERO such references, in every grid arm and in every golden
database read here.
