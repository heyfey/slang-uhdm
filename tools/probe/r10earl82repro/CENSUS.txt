THE THREE POPULATIONS THE BRIEF ASKED FOR, read off `g5_enc82.sv` (the grid) and
confirmed by the corpus:

 (a) GOLDEN IS THE DECLARED WIDTH, OURS 64 — a range bound naming a parameter
     that is NOT overridden and IS explicitly typed (`int`->32, `byte`->8,
     `shortint`->16, `longint`->64, `logic [7:0]`->8, `bit [3:0]`->4), or one
     that IS overridden through a bare-NAME actual.  This is the population the
     law moves.

 (b) BOTH ARE 64 — the MUST-NOT-MOVE set, and it is exactly earl81's shape: a
     parameter OVERRIDDEN with a BARE DECIMAL (`.DBARE(2)`), a PARENTHESISED
     bare decimal (`(8)`), or an ARITHMETIC actual (`4+4`).  `collectParams_`
     stores those with no adjustSize, so Surelog's Value is the unsized 64 and
     so is ours.  g5's OVI / OVU / OVB / OVX / OVW, and the HOP chain whose ROOT
     actual is the bare decimal 40.

 (c) THE THIRD RULE, and it is neither: an UNTYPED parameter, and a BASED-literal
     initialiser.  An untyped one answers 32 at some consumers and 64 at others
     in golden (g5's PKU: 3 x sz32 on the typespec copy, 6 x sz64 elsewhere), and
     a based literal keeps its OWN base and width (`4'h7` -> HEX:7 sz4 ct5,
     `8'd8` -> INT:8 sz8 ct7).  Neither is modelled; both are REFUSED by name in
     the law's fences and left byte-identical.
