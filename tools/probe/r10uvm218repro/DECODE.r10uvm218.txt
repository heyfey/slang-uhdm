THE MODEL, AND WHY IT IS THE CLASS-METHOD WINDOW
------------------------------------------------
That is the SAME state § classMethodNoBind describes for a method written
inside a class and § unitSubNoBind9 for a `$unit`-scope subroutine: the body is
compiled before any symbol table exists and is never revisited.  So the set
that still binds is ROAD 1's set, and ROAD 1's set IS the class-method binding
window — which is why this law is a GATED EXTENSION of the buvm window rather
than a new rule.  Arm a2 (`grid218/a2_window`) measures all thirty-one cells of
it on ONE golden and every carve-out the campaign already decoded holds

AND THE HALF THAT IS NOT THE CLASS WINDOW: THE SYMBOL
------------------------------------------------------
An in-body class method's window is a POSITION test.  Here the SYMBOL matters
too, and arm a2 is what said so: the interface's OWN member variable (w03
29:11), member net (w04 30:11) and localparam (w05 31:11) are golden-BARE
inside a condition, and the same three as call-statement arguments (w19 52:22,
w20 53:22, w21 54:17) are golden-bare as well, while a FORMAL and a subroutine
LOCAL in those very positions bind.  Road 1 explains it exactly: `bindVariable`
walks the enclosing `uhdmfunction`/`uhdmtask` scope's own `Variables()` and
`Io_decls()` and STOPS — it never reaches the interface's members.  That is why
the law is NOT simply `classMethodNoBind = true`, and why the predicate carries
an explicit declaring-scope test.
