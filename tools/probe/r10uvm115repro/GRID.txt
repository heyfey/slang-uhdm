THE GENERATE-SCOPE CONJUNCT, and the arm that forced it.
`g115_d.sv` cells `ifp` / `unp` are the decisive pair: golden gives the SAME
declaration `ref_var + unsupported_typespec` in the module's DEF listing and
`class_var + class_typespec 'process'` in the ELABORATED listing.  That is the
phase-order law one phase further down — a generate scope's content is built
during ELABORATION, after `Builtin::addBuiltinClasses`, while the DEF listing
compiles the generate construct in the module pass (and holds `gen_if` /
`named_begin`, never a `gen_scope`).  The gate needs no view flag: a `gen_scope`
on the parent chain IS the elaborated generate scope.  r10uvm77's committed arm
v77x4_scope.sv cell (5) is that cell, and the BOUNDARY GATE convicted a first
cut that moved it away.  With the conjunct in, arm d agrees with golden on
every one of its process cells in both views, and v77x4_scope /
r10oth7repro's c3_bounds move only TOWARD golden (11 -> 5 and 16 -> 10
differing cells, 0 regressions).

THE SECOND CONJUNCT — golden binds NO `ref_obj` to a `ref_var` local.
Measured on golden's own dump (arm a `fn115` / `fn115b`, and every corpus
site): `p = process::self();` has an UNBOUND `ref_obj` Lhs on golden's side
while the SIBLING declarations `cs` / `top` (real design classes) ARE bound.
Decoded: `UhdmWriter::lateBinding` (UhdmWriter.cpp:3294) refuses every
candidate whose kind is `uhdmref_var` — the test appears eight times in that
one function (:3315, :3324, :3335, :3344, :3387, :3397, :3418, :3426).  Our
emitter inherits the refusal for free once the declaration IS a `ref_var`:
measured over SimpleUVM's 16 family REF cells, 15 now match golden EXACTLY
(both views) where 5 did before.

THE -nobuiltin CONTROL (measured, `surelog -nobuiltin` + `slang-uhdm
--nobuiltin` on g115_c.sv): golden's IN-BODY class-method local `kp` becomes
`ref_var + unsupported_typespec 'process'` there too — which is the phase-order
law running with `Builtin::addBuiltinClasses` switched OFF, and is the direct
experimental confirmation of the decode.  We keep `class_var` in that mode
(residue R2, zero corpus witnesses in either corpus).
