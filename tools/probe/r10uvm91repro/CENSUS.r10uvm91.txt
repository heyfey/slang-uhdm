C.  THE REGRESSION THE CENSUS CAUGHT BEFORE ANY GATE RAN
=========================================================================
The FIRST cut of this law had no `dimensions.empty()` test on the typedef
candidate walk.  The depth-keyed census read back SIX pristine-right ->
patched-wrong cells, by name, in one run:

  uvm_pkg::uvm_spell_chkr | IO check::strtab | G array_typespec 'tab_t'
                                             | PRIS ok -> PAT int_typespec
  uvm_pkg::uvm_spell_chkr | LISTING          | same
  uvm_pkg::uvm_spell_chkr | TYPEPARAM T      | G int_typespec '' (ANONYMOUS)
                                             | PRIS ok -> PAT 'tab_t'
  uvm_pkg::uvm_utils      | LISTING          | G array_typespec 'types_t'
  uvm_pkg::uvm_utils      | RET find_all     | same
  uvm_pkg::uvm_utils      | TYPEPARAM TYPE   | G int_typespec '' -> 'types_t'

`uvm_spell_chkr #(type T=int)` writes `typedef T tab_t[string];` and
`uvm_utils #(type TYPE=int)` writes `typedef TYPE types_t[$];`.  A DIMENSIONED
typedef is an `array_typespec` OVER the parameter, not a typedef OF it, and it
names NOTHING: golden leaves those type_parameters ANONYMOUS.  Arm cells
`c_dim` / `c_dim2` were written afterwards to hold the corpus's own lesson, and
the gate is `tds.dimensions.empty()` on the candidate arm — a test
§ classTpGroup87's transcription applies to its CONSUMPTION arm only.
