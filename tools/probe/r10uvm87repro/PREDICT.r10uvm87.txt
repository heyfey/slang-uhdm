==========================================================================
SITE B — the DECLARATION sites (properties, array elements, body locals,
         nested-block locals, return vars)          (SOURCE, then measured)
==========================================================================
Site B fires only where a class-scope declaration is WRITTEN as one of the
class's own type parameters (or a typedef of one) AND that group has a WINNING
TYPEDEF (§ classTypeParamTdGroupLastWins33c's winner).  A group with no typedef
is left exactly as it was — golden names its objects for the class, which is
already what the pristine emitter does (arm a2 `c_notd`, 5/5 cells).

The corpus reach is therefore bounded by the source predicate
`typedef <TYPE PARAMETER> <name>;` inside a class.  Over the whole uvm-1.2 tree
