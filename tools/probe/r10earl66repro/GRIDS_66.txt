GRID B — WHAT ENCODING does the fold carry?  (g1_enum66.sv, g2_enc66.sv)
         golden's constant, ELAB High_conn bit-select index
=====================================================================
  member declaration            golden ct  sz   value        decompile
  ---------------------------------------------------------------------
  EH0 = 3'h0                          5    3    HEX:0        3'h0
  EH4 = 3'h4                          5    3    HEX:4        3'h4
  EB2 = 4'b0010                       3    4    BIN:0010     4'b0010
  ED5 = 8'd5                          7    8    INT:5        5
  XS  = 8'sd9                         7    8    INT:9        9
  EU1 = 1        (untyped base)       9    64   UINT:1       1
  LU1 = 1        (module-local, untyped) 9  64   UINT:1      1
  EA2            (auto, logic [2:0])  7    3    INT:2        2
  EI1            (auto, int base)     7    32   INT:1        1
  EM4            (auto after 8'h3, bit[7:0]) 7 8 INT:4       4
  LH2 = 3'h2     (module-local hex)   5    3    HEX:2        3'h2
  UN1 = 2'h2     ($unit enum)         5    2    HEX:2        2'h2
  XP  = PLP[2:0] (from a localparam)  3    3    BIN:101      3'b101
  XA  = 3'h2+3'h1 (EXPRESSION-valued) 9    3    UINT:3       3    (*)
  XO  = 5'o17    (OCTAL)              9    5    UINT:15      15   (*)

  THE RULE: value and size are the ENUM MEMBER'S OWN (`enum_const::VpiValue`
  / `VpiSize`); vpiConstType comes from the value's ENCODING PREFIX
  (BIN->3 OCT->4 HEX->5 DEC->1 INT->7 UINT->9); the decompile is RE-MINTED
  from the value -- bare DIGITS for INT:/UINT:, `<size>'<base><digits>`
  otherwise.  Copying the member's own VpiDecompile is WRONG for `8'd5`
  (member "8'd5", fold "5").

  (*) The two rows marked (*) are cells where OUR enum_const's own value
  differs from Surelog's before this pass ever runs (XA: golden UINT:3, ours
  BIN:011; XO: golden UINT:15, ours OCT:17).  They were WRONG before the fold
  (ref_obj where golden has a constant) and they are WRONG after it (a
  constant with our encoding) -- a SEPARATE law, ranked R2 in PREDICT_R0.txt,
  and there is no cell of that shape anywhere in either corpus (fence scan).
