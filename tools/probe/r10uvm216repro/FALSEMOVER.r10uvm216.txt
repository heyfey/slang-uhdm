The residue this arm exposes is recorded as R2 in the README: when the export
is written BELOW its definition (`epsil7`, definition :55, export :58) golden

EMPTY `vpiAccessType=EXPORT` prototype at the export's slot.  We emit ONE,
stamped.  That is unchanged pristine to patched and no corpus row has that
shape (every corpus export is written ABOVE or ADJACENT to its definition), so
it is recorded, not taken.
