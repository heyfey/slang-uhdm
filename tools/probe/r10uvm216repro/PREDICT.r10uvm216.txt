THE LAW
-------
§ dpiExportListedAtExportItem216 — an `export "DPI-C"` item creates the
subroutine's listing entry AT THE EXPORT ITEM'S POSITION and stamps it
(`vpiAccessType = vpiDPIExportAcc`, `vpiDPICStr = vpiDPIC`, the EXPORT's span);
the later definition fills that same object in place.

Two consequences, so two ways a row can move:
  (P) POSITION — only when the export item and the definition item are NOT
      adjacent in the holder's item list.
  (F) FLAGS — only in a holder whose body slang never elaborated during
      `Compilation::elaborate()`, i.e. a never-instantiated INTERFACE
      definition (slang auto-instantiates only modules and programs as tops).
